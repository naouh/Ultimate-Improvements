# Getting Started

DartCraft progression is short but specific. Here's the path from spawn to a fully upgraded Force Pickaxe.

## 1. Find Power Ore

**Power Ore** generates up to Y=48 in the Overworld and throughout the Nether. Mine it with a stone pickaxe or better. Each block drops a **Force Gem** (plus extra with Fortune).

Power Ore also has a chance to drop directly from dungeon loot, scrapbox drops (with IC2), and macerating (also IC2).

## 2. Smelt or craft your way to Force Ingots

You need **Force Ingots** to make tools. Several routes:

- **Smelt** 8 Force Wood planks → 2× Golden Power Source. (Not ingots, but used in furnace fuel recipes.)
- **Vanilla recipe**: 1 Force Gem + 2 Iron Ingots → 2 Force Ingots (shapeless).
- **Ore Dict** (any present): Force Gem + 2× ingotBronze → 2 ingots; Force Gem + 2× ingotRefinedIron / ingotSilver / xychoridite → 3 ingots.
- **Forestry Carpenter**: 2×2 or 1×2 Iron in 750 mB Liquid Force → 2 Force Ingots; same with Bronze/Refined Iron/Silver for 2–3.
- **Crafting grid**: 9 Force Nuggets → 1 Force Ingot (and back: 1 ingot shapeless → 9 nuggets).

## 3. Plant a Force Tree (optional but recommended)

Force saplings can be found in dungeons or generated by world-gen. Plant on dirt/grass.

Trees grow into **Force Wood** with **Force Leaves**. Strip a log to get **Force Planks** (1 Force Wood → 4 Planks). 1 Force Plank → 8 **Force Sticks**. Force Sticks are required for every Force tool handle.

Bonemealing a Force Sapling has a chance to produce **Force Flax** drops (from leaves) that gives **Tears**.

## 4. Craft a Force Rod

The Force Rod is the **upgrade applicator** — once a tool is infused, you point at it with a Force Rod to apply tool-specific actions like renaming, picking up XP, etc.

Recipe (any of):
```
  I       I = Force Ingot
 S        S = Force Stick (or vanilla Stick for a 48-durability rod)
N         N = Force Nugget
```

## 5. Craft your first Force tool

Standard tool recipes work — replace iron with Force Ingot, stick with Force Stick:

| Tool | Pattern |
|---|---|
| Force Sword | I/I/S |
| Force Pickaxe | III / .S. / .S. |
| Force Shovel | I / S / S |
| Force Axe | II / IS / .S |
| Force Shears | F. / .F (two Force Ingots, diagonal) |
| Force Bow | strung with Force Sticks + 3 String |

See [recipes.md](recipes.md) for the exact crafting grids.

## 6. Build a Force Infuser

The infuser is the centerpiece. Recipe is in [recipes.md](recipes.md). Place it down and right-click to open the GUI.

Slots:
- **Slot 0** (left): Force Tome (drives infusion progress and gates upgrade tiers).
- **Slot 1** (top middle): Force Gem / Force Shard / Force Bucket / Force Container → fills the internal tank with Liquid Force.
  - Force Gem = 1000 mB
  - Force Shard = 1000 mB **+ 10 bonus tome points** (if a tome is present)
  - Force Bucket = 1000 mB (leaves an empty bucket)
- **Slot 2** (target): the tool / armor / pack / rod / tome / upgrade core you want to infuse.
- **Slots 3–10** (8 material slots): the upgrade materials (see [force-infusions.md](force-infusions.md)).

Power source: BuildCraft MJ — attach a Stirling Engine, Combustion Engine, or a DartCraft **Force Engine**.

## 7. Make a Force Tome

Force Tome can be crafted (see recipes) **or** transmuted from a vanilla Book using a Force Rod on it (see Transmutations in [items.md](items.md)).

Without a tome, you can still infuse — but the bonus XP is dropped on the floor instead of stored, and you can't unlock higher tiers.

## 8. Infusing — tiers in practice

The tome starts at **Tier 1**. To unlock more upgrades and bigger Force Packs, gain points by infusing:

| Tier | Points required |
|---:|---:|
| 1 | 0 |
| 2 | 96 |
| 3 | 270 |
| 4 | 600 |
| 5 | 1020 |
| 6 | 1440 |
| 7 (Mastered) | 2400 |

Points per material applied scale with the upgrade's tier (1 / 5 / 15 / 25 / 50 per tier). Every applied upgrade also adds +25 points the first time. A Force Shard fed into Slot 1 adds 10 bonus points to the active tome.

> Hold **SHIFT** over a tome to see current points and points-to-next.

## 9. Power the Infuser

Each infusion uses energy and Liquid Force scaled by the materials' efficiency:

- **Energy**: 500 MJ × (sum of material efficiencies)
- **Liquid Force**: 200 mB × (sum of material efficiencies)
- **Speed**: 5 MJ/T → a 1.0-efficiency infusion takes ~5 seconds

The Force Engine is your built-in option:

- Fuel slot (bottom): **Liquid Force** (4.0× modifier, 60 000 ticks/bucket), **Oil**, **BioFuel**, **Fuel**, or **Lava**.
- Throttle slot (top): **Water** (2.0×), **Ice** (4.0×), or **Milk** (2.5×). Throttle is the cooling/multiplier liquid; you need both for the engine to cycle.

The Force Engine outputs MJ on its facing direction — point it at a Force Infuser or a BC pipe.

## 10. Suggested first-hour goals

1. Craft a Force Pickaxe + Sword + Shovel + Axe.
2. Find or make 16 Force Gems → enough to brick-up an Infuser and start filling its tank.
3. Build a Force Engine, fuel it with Liquid Force (or oil from BC).
4. Get a tome. Slot pick + 1 Force Nugget into the Infuser → free Force I infusion to learn the GUI.
5. Stack early upgrades: **Heat** (charcoal) on the pickaxe, **Speed** (sugar) on Sword, **Storage** on a Force Pack.
6. Push the tome to Tier 3 to start using **Touch** (silk touch from cobweb) and **Healing** (ghast tear).
7. Mastered tome (Tier 7) unlocks the final unique upgrades like **Wing** (flight, feather) and **Ender**.
